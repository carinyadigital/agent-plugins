# Review content calendar

## Before running

1. Read the calendar at `docs/content/content-calendar.md`.
2. Resolve brand per [../../../references/content-conventions.md](../../../references/content-conventions.md).
3. Load `brand/seasonal-calendar.md` and `brand/brand-voice.md`.

## Task

Review the calendar for completeness, brand alignment, and executability.

### Checklist

- [ ] Monthly theme matches seasonal events for the month
- [ ] Post and recipe counts match squad cadence
- [ ] Each slot has slug, type, brief, and status
- [ ] Briefs are specific enough for `draft-post` / `draft-recipe`
- [ ] No scheduling conflicts or duplicate slugs
- [ ] SEO keyword targets noted where research exists

## Output

Structured review with **pass** or **revise** verdict. List blocking issues and
suggested edits. Do not rewrite the full calendar unless asked.
